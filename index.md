# Privacy Policy for Charging Alarm

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Charging Alarm ("the app") is developed by Arohn Bermudez.

## Information we collect

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It does not require an account, and it does not send any data to our servers or to third parties.

## Permissions

The app may use device permissions (such as notifications) only to alert you about your device's charging status. This information stays on your device.

## Third-party services

The app does not use advertising or analytics services.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
